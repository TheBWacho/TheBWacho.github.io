Embed & Publish to Google Sites

Google Sites blocks external web uploads via API, so embedding takes one click to copy the complete self-contained HTML and paste it straight into your Google Site:

  1. Click Copy Embed Code & Open Google Sites below (copies this entire app's HTML to your clipboard and opens sites.google.com).
  2. Open your site in Google Sites and go to Insert → Embed → Embed code.
  3. Press Ctrl+V to paste, click Next → Insert, stretch the frame full-width, and hit Publish.
